Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Asta is a private assistant app. Its only user is its owner, who signs in with their own Google account. This policy explains what Asta does with the Google data that the owner allows it to reach.

What Asta can access

Only the Google services the owner approves on Google's consent screen. These can include Google Sheets, Google Docs, Google Drive, Google Calendar and read-only contacts. Gmail is only included if the owner approves it.

How Asta uses it

Asta uses Google data only to carry out the owner's own requests, such as reading or updating the owner's spreadsheets, documents, files and calendar events. It does not use Google data for advertising. It does not sell it, and it does not share it with anyone other than the service provider described below.

Where data goes

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own server and computer, readable only by the owner's account.
  • To answer a request, the relevant content is sent to the AI model provider that the owner has chosen. The provider handles it under the owner's account settings with that provider.
  • Asta keeps nothing from Google beyond what a request needs, except notes the owner asks it to save.

Removing access

The owner can remove Asta's access at any time from Google Account permissions. Removing access stops all further use of Google data.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

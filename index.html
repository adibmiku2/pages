Asta

A private assistant app with one user: its owner.

Asta runs on its owner's own server. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

With the owner's permission, Asta reads and updates the owner's own Google Sheets, Docs, Drive files, Calendar events and contacts. It uses them to keep personal planning spreadsheets and schedules up to date when the owner asks.

How Asta handles Google data is set out in the privacy policy.

Asta is a personal project and is not affiliated with Google.
